# Puzzle Piece — "Four circles" (v3, from scratch)

## PAGE
- **Problem:** a warning tells an autistic child *when*, never *what happens next*; Puzzle Piece makes social stories that rehearse the moment, with the child in it.
- **Audience:** parents of autistic kids on a vertical feed (Reels/TikTok/Shorts), sound often on for a first-person voice, tired of "apps" and pity ads.
- **Goal:** start the free account (story ideas + his character before paying; 7-day trial).
- **Emotion:** from the knot of "leaving the park" to warmth and relief: "a way to prepare together."
- One true thing: the parent describes the hard part in plain words, and gets a story starring their child, beside the people he knows. The voice is the user's script, read verbatim (ElevenLabs connector, "Juliet", take A of 2, 60.9 s); every cut is placed on its measured word times.

## Ideas
1. *(first, set aside)* puzzle pieces snapping into the logo: the name's first association.
2. *(set aside, already made in v2)* a UI documentary of the sign-up on a 3D phone.
3. **Four circles.** The logo's four circles are the cast: the green circle is David, the orange is his mum, teal and red are the world (the gate, the car). Act 1 tells the problem as a tiny geometric social story with them. On "That's why we use Puzzle Piece" the family comes home and the piece closes around them: the mark. Act 2: the circles become windows onto the real product. The green circle fills with storybook David, the orange with his mum, four circles hold the four art styles, and the mark is rebuilt at the end.
4. A typographic essay: "when" against "what".

**Pick: 3.** Cover the logo and the film still says Puzzle Piece: the cast *is* the mark (its exact colours, overlap and construction) and the ending is the mark's own geometry. It also says something true about the product: the child is put into the story beside the people he knows.

## World (one for the whole film)
- **Ground:** warm cream paper with fibre grain, key light from the top left, falloff to the lower right.
- **Light/depth:** the discs are paper cut-outs with soft contact shadows; where they overlap they multiply, exactly like the logo. The real UI and art sit on the same paper, as lit cards.
- **Texture:** paper grain overlay; 180° shutter motion blur plus light grain at render.
- **Sound:** the mother's voice carries the film. Under it is a quiet felt-piano / music-box bed, ducked under the voice: a tight pulse at the countdown, a three-note walk for the gate, car and home, and a warm chord each time the mark closes. UI ticks and the page turn are dry and close. Silence before "meltdown."
