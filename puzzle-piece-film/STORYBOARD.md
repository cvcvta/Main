# Puzzle Piece — "The missing piece" (30 s hero explainer)

## PAGE
- **Problem:** parents of autistic kids already give warnings and countdowns; a warning says *when*, never *what happens next*. Puzzle Piece's personalised social stories fill that gap.
- **Audience:** a parent of an autistic child scrolling a feed, likely muted at first, tired, sceptical of "apps". Sound-on is a bonus, not a requirement, so the picture and on-screen words carry the story alone.
- **Goal:** start the free sign-up (story ideas and the child's character before paying, 7-day trial).
- **Emotion:** from the dread of "leaving the park" to relief and calm: "a way to prepare together".

Assumption, stated: the script is 188 words. Read aloud that is about 65-75 s, and as on-screen text it is about 6 words/s, which nobody can read. The film is held to the 30 s brief. Every paragraph of the script gets its own beat, in order, and the on-screen copy is the script's own words, trimmed to the phrase that carries each paragraph. The beat table is data, so a full VO cut can be retimed later.

## Ideas
1. (first, discarded) Puzzle pieces fly together into the logo over "Puzzle Piece". Name's first association, says nothing about transitions.
2. **UI documentary of David's real sign-up**, framed by the problem: real copy and pictures from the recordings, magnified by the camera.
3. **"When vs what"**: a countdown (the warning) only counts *when*; a story strip of interlocking jigsaw panels (gate, car, drive home) shows *what*. The logo's own shape is the joint between moments.

**Pick:** 3 as the opening act, flowing into 2 for the product. Cover-the-logo test: the interlocking panels and the 4 overlapping circles are both this brand's own mark doing a job, and the UI is theirs. A competitor swap would lose the mark, the illustrations and the real copy.

## World
- **Ground:** warm cream paper (#F6F4EA) with fibre grain and a soft key light from the top left. One world for the whole film: the UI is the same paper.
- **Light:** a warm key at the top left with falloff to the bottom right; contact shadows under cards. The brand colour appears as light only in the ending's overlapping circles.
- **Depth:** 3 planes: drifting translucent brand circles far back (very low, out of focus), the lit subject, and a foreground blur on camera pushes.
- **Texture:** paper grain overlay; the ink drawings are line art with a little wobble.
- **Sound:** a procedural score: a soft felt-piano / music-box bed, a tense low pulse under the countdown, a dip to near silence before "what happens next", warm chords from the product act on, three hand-placed hits (the pieces locking, the logo landing, the final mark). UI ticks on presses and typing.

## Hero moment (screenshot frame)
~8.6 s: three jigsaw story panels (the gate, the car, the drive home), ink drawn on paper and lit, locking edge to edge across the frame while the camera tracks. Second hero: 17-19 s, one storybook scene of David and his mum cycling through LEGO, Minecraft, Pixar and Simpsons styles with a moving wipe, then splitting into the four labelled tiles.
