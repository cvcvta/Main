# personaos: "One hire" (motionmaxxing recreation of the voiceover cut)

A second version of `../reel-vo.html`, built with the motionmaxxing method to compare against it. Same voiceover
(`../assets/vo.mp3` with the pauses in `../assets/vo-edit.json`), same paper-white ground, no music, same search-bar
outro the user asked for. What changes is the direction: one continuous world instead of seven separate scenes,
every scene handing its object to the next, measured eases, oversize arrivals, a physical cursor, and captions set at
speech rhythm in one fixed place instead of a new headline layout per scene.

## PAGE
- **P Problem:** an HVAC owner pays an overseas agency thousands a month and still has empty calendar slots.
- **A Audience:** HVAC company owners scrolling Reels or TikTok on a phone, often muted, sceptical of agencies.
- **G Goal:** tap the link and get started with Mo.
- **E Emotion:** from irritated (money leaving) to relieved and a little charmed (a hire that just works).
- **One true thing:** Mo writes and runs the ads, and the result is booked jobs on the calendar.

## Ideas
1. **First idea (put aside):** a feature tour, one headline + one card per line of the voiceover. That is the current cut,
   and the category's film: swap the logo and it advertises any AI marketing tool.
2. **A joke with a character:** Mo interviews for the job against the agency; the agency's invoice argues back.
   Charming, but the voiceover is a straight read, so the joke would fight it.
3. **A UI documentary of one hire (picked):** follow one object through the whole film. The agency's invoice is struck
   out; Mo lands where it was and becomes the face of an HVAC thermostat (72°); the thermostat becomes the image in the ad
   Mo writes; a customer taps the ad's "Book now" and that button flies into the calendar as the first booked job; the
   calendar runs past midnight to a 2:14 AM booking (24/7); Mo jokes "Raise? Nah."; ideas spill out of him; he
   shrinks into the photo on his own staff badge; the badge's "Hire Mo" button gets clicked and stretches into the
   browser's address bar, where personaos.com types and turns into the logo.
   **Why:** every joint is caused by the product's own gestures (write, run, book, hire, click), Mo is the carrier and
   never sits still in one place for more than two shots, and the brand's real CTA ("Hire Mo") and success state
   (BOOKED, lime) do the work. Cover the logo and it is still about hiring Mo, not about "AI marketing".

## World (one act, constant ground, as the user asked)
- **Ground:** paper white #FAFAF7 with a fine paper tooth, never changing colour.
- **Light:** one soft key from the top left; every object casts a contact shadow and a soft ambient shadow down-right.
- **Depth:** objects on paper (invoice, ad, calendar, badge, bar), Mo floating above them with his floor shadow;
  bills and idea chips as a near plane.
- **Texture:** paper tooth in the ground, 3 % temporal grain, 180° shutter blur (one blur grammar: shutter only).
- **Sound:** the voiceover; close, dry UI sounds on the events that cause things (strike, press, booking, keys); no music.
- **Picture source:** all code-drawn in the brand's grammar (Mo is the site's own render); the ad, calendar and badge
  are illustrative rebuilds in the product's colours, declared in NOTE.md.
- **Screenshot-worthy hero moment (11.0 s):** the week calendar, cropped by the frame edges, filling with lime BOOKED
  jobs in a cascade while the counter steps to 16, the first job still trailing in from the "Book now" press.

## Captions
The voiceover's words at speech rhythm (each word lands 2 f before it is heard), one or two lines, always centred at the
same height near the top. The one size spike is "Mo." (spent once). Captions are kept because Reels are watched muted.

## Gates planned before building
- **G1:** the readable proof per beat is the invoice total (190 px), the ad copy (54 px), the counter "16 jobs booked"
  (64 px), the 2:14 AM tile (68 px after the snap), the badge name (130 px), the URL (64 px). Calendar job titles are
  texture at 25 px (below 0.04 H), declared.
- **G3:** last cut at the click (30.45 s), final shot about 2.1 s of 32.6 s; the logo keeps drifting, so no frozen end.
- **G4:** one hero per frame: the caption sits above the hero, never beside it.
- **Deliberate exceptions the user asked for:** the letter-by-letter typing (it is the browser's own input, which the
  skill allows) and the search-bar-to-logo transformation; both are the requested outro.
