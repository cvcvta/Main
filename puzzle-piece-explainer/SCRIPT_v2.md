# Puzzle Piece: "Except Mine" (v2, ~44s)

I told him about the fire drill. I showed him a video. I even played the alarm on my phone so he'd know the sound.
The next day, every kid in his class walked outside. Except mine.
Ryan's autistic. When that alarm goes off, it's not practice. It's an emergency.
Telling him what's coming doesn't show him what it looks like.
His therapist suggested a social story: a short story that walks him through the moment before it happens.
Great idea. Except I can't draw.
Then I found Puzzle Piece.
I told it what was hard. It made a story about Ryan, with his class and his teacher, his favorite art style, and read it to him in my voice.
We read it every night that week. And at the next fire drill, he walked outside with his class.
It doesn't replace his therapist. It gives him the words before he needs them.

- The video, the phone alarm, "every night that week" and the fire-drill outcome must reflect a real family's experience. Otherwise label the ad "Dramatization".
