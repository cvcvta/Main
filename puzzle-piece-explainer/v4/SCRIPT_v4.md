# Puzzle Piece: "Except Mine" v4 script (personalization)

I told him about the fire drill. I showed him a video. I even played the alarm on my phone so he'd know the sound.
The next day, every kid in his class walked outside. Except mine.
Ryan's autistic. When that alarm goes off, it's not practice. It's an emergency.
Telling him what's coming doesn't show him what it looks like.
His therapist suggested a social story: a short story that walks him through the moment before it happens.
Social stories are meant to be personal, written for one child, about their world.
Great idea. Except I can't draw.
Then I found Puzzle Piece's My Story Time. It makes them as personal as it gets.
I uploaded a photo of his dad, and one of me, so we show up in his stories, looking like us.
I told it what was hard. It made a story starring Ryan, with his class, his teacher, his favorite art style, and a world he loves.
It even reads it to him in my voice, and he follows along with the highlighted words. Every story saves to his library, so he can read it again and again.
We read it every night that week. And at the next fire drill, he walked outside with his class.
It doesn't replace his therapist. It gives him the words before he needs them.

## Claim note
The request was to say that studies show the more personalized a social story is, the better it performs. I couldn't find a study that tested this:
- Kokina & Kern's 2010 meta-analysis reports low to questionable overall effectiveness. The summaries I found list participant age, diagnosis and story format as moderators, not personalization.
- A pilot RCT of digitally mediated social stories (PMC7677143) used stories personalized to each child but did not test personalization as a separate factor.

What is supported is that social stories are individualized by design. That is why the script says "meant to be personal, written for one child, about their world" and "as personal as it gets" rather than "studies show…" or "the most personalized platform". If the team has a specific study, the stronger line can go back in.
