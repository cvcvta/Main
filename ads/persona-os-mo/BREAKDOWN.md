Source: 720×1280 (9:16), 30 fps, 33.27 s, H.264 + AAC stereo. Continuous VO over a music bed
(no silence gaps > 0.25 s). Kinetic captions track the VO word-by-word. Cut times below come from
ffmpeg scene detection (`select=gt(scene,0.15)`) checked against 2 fps contact sheets.

Visual system: a condensed, wide grotesk (squared "M"/"W") in white + neon lime on dark scenes,
and royal-blue on light scenes. Words pop in one at a time with motion blur, slight 3D tilt and a
fade from 40% grey to full colour. Film grain and a vignette sit on every shot.

## Beat structure (what the remake keeps)

1. **Hook question** that challenges a hiring decision.
2. **Reframe**: there's a faster way.
3. **Hidden costs** of the hire, as a stacking list.
4. **Risk**: what happens when the hire leaves.
5. **Brand reveal** on a dark, glowing stage: you get a whole team.
6. **Team roles** orbiting the logo.
7. **Coverage over time** (calendar grid filling up).
8. **"The part no employee can offer"** turn.
9. **Flexibility** sequence around a glowing object.
10. **Three "No ___" lines.**
11. **Qualifier**: who this is for.
12. **CTA**.
