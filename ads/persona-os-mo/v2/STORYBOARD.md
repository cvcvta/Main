# Persona OS — "Hire Mo" v2 storyboard (9:16, 30 fps, ~35.4 s, cut to the existing VO)

**PAGE.** Problem: owners do their own marketing at night or try to hire for it. Audience: small-business
owners (home services first, per personaos.com). Goal: tap through to hire Mo from $49. Emotion: relief.

**Inventory (from personaos.com).** Mark: the four-point sparkle + "personaos". Character: Mo, a glassy
violet orb with two eyes. Core verb: *hire*. Success moment: "Design visit confirmed. 10:00 AM". Real
UI on the site: the Stone & Oak website, "New guide published", "SEO updated", the October content
calendar with STATIC / VIDEO / MEME posts, the "Do you do bathrooms in Austin?" comment flagged as a
potential customer, the Sarah text thread that books a design visit. Colour logic: off-white paper,
ink, one purple (#6938F5) that means *Mo is doing this*. Type: Helvetica/Inter, sentence case, medium
weight, purple second line. Would never: neon, shouting all-caps, stock "AI" sparkles everywhere.

**The idea.** *Mo takes the empty chair.* The film is one continuous camera move through one office:
the hire costs pile up on the man, he vanishes (match cut to the empty desk), Mo drops into his seat,
and the camera dives through Mo's glass into everything Mo makes. Mo is the carried object across
every seam and arrives by cause (filling the seat; landing in the owner's hands at the end), never as
a logo sting. Cover the logo and it still only works for an AI-employee brand whose mascot is an orb.

**One system.** Smooth 30 fps clock, 4-subframe shutter blur on everything, expo-out landings (most
travel in the first 6 frames, long tail, no bounce), expo-in exits. Words unmask upward from a line
mask at speech rhythm, landing 1-2 frames before they're heard. One spring in the whole film (Mo
landing in the chair). Purple = Mo's work. Light grain, one soft key light per world.
