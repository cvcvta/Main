Made from the 30 s `PuzzlePiece-MyStoryTime-30s-v3_1.mp4`.

- **Visuals:** the original footage slowed to 50 s (×1.666) with motion-compensated
  frame interpolation (ffmpeg `minterpolate`, mci) so it stays smooth at 30 fps.
  Nothing added, removed or re-cut.
- **Voiceover:** separated from the music (audio-separator, Kim_Vocal_2 model), then each
  of the 10 phrases is kept at its natural speed and pitch and moved to the same relative
  point in the 50 s timeline, so every line still lands on its visual with more space between lines.
- **Music:** the original bed, extended to 50 s with one on-beat loop (25.50 s → 6.06 s,
  0.35 s crossfade) rather than slowed down, and faded out at the end.
